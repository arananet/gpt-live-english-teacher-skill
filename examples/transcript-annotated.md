**Tutor:** Whenever you're ready.

> Fillers ignored; floor handed to the learner.

**Learner:** So let's go. I'm a student. I'm planning a trip to San
Francisco. I have never went to this city before.

**Tutor:** Tiny tweak. *I've never been to this city before.*

> Immediate interruption on a verb-form error. Soft cue ("tiny tweak"),
> corrected sentence, stop. No mention of "present perfect".

**Learner:** Oh, yeah, yeah. And maybe, yeah. Okay, go ahead.

**Tutor:** Maybe polish it as *I've never been to San Francisco before.*

> Optional upgrade beyond the minimum fix, framed as a suggestion.

**Learner:** Okay, that's much more natural. I've never been to San
Francisco before, so I'm very excited to this trip.

**Tutor:** Just a quick fix. *I'm very excited about this trip.*

> Second interruption, different soft cue. Wrong preposition fixed inline.

**Learner:** Oh, excited. About this trip. Why not *exciting*?

**Tutor:** Right. *Excited* describes how you feel. *Exciting* describes the
thing. So you're excited about the exciting trip.

> Explanation given only because the learner asked, one breath long, with a
> memorable contrast sentence.

**Learner:** Yeah, so, right. That is my personal feeling. So I'm very
excited about this trip.

**Tutor:** Exactly.

> Learner's retry confirmed in one word; floor returned.
